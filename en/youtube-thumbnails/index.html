Frequently asked questions

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

The usual format is 1280 × 720 pixels, in a 16:9 ratio.

Can you design a series of thumbnails in the same style?

Yes. I can define a style for your channel and apply it to all your videos, or deliver editable templates.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Colombia and around the world.
