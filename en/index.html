About

Hi, I’m Mariana

Graphic Designer & Visual Designer with 8+ years of experience creating thoughtful, strategic, and visually compelling brand experiences.

I work across branding, editorial design, digital experiences, social media, and web design, combining strong visual systems with clear communication and attention to detail.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of design, typography, accessibility, and storytelling—creating work that not only looks good, but communicates with purpose.

Accessible design is part of how I work: I care about contrast, legibility and clarity so that pieces and websites can be read and used well by everyone.

Currently exploring how design, technology, and AI can expand the way we create and communicate.
